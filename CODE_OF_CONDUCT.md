# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, kind and respectful, whatever someone's background, experience, identity or
language. Harassment, insults, personal attacks, sexualised content and publishing other people's
private information are not acceptable, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the
project is represented.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break these rules, and may block
people who repeatedly do.

**Reporting:** contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/devgalib) or the address on their profile. Reports are handled
confidentially.
